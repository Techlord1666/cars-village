Company Policies

Privacy

At Cars Village Global, we prioritize your security. We collect your data solely for account management and transaction verification. All payment data is encrypted via industry-standard protocols.

Global Shipping

We offer worldwide shipping to major ports. Quotes are calculated based on destination and vehicle weight. Customers are responsible for local import duties and taxes.

Delivery

Within Nigeria, we offer doorstep delivery via specialized car haulers. Lagos delivery takes 24-48 hours. Nationwide typically takes 3-7 business days.
